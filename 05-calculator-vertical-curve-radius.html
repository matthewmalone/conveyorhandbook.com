1. Calculator 5. Vertical Curve Radius

Finds the radius a concave (valley) or convex (crest) curve needs on a flat or troughed belt, from lift-off, edge buckling, edge tension and centre tension in each load case, with the governing radius highlighted. It also checks a nominal radius, draws the curve to scale, shows the tension across the belt width and compares troughing angles.
