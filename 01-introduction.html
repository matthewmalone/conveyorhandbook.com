1. Introduction

This handbook provides practical guidance for the mechanical, structural and operational design of bulk materials handling belt conveyors, drawing on the author’s design and maintenance experience, international standards (AS, ISO, DIN and CEMA) and empirical data.

The author provides independent conveyor consulting, system optimisation and design services. Enquiries: hello@conveyorhandbook.com

2. Key Design Principle

Eliminate components before optimising them. The simplest system that meets the duty is the most reliable.

4. Disclaimer of Liability and Engineering Responsibility
