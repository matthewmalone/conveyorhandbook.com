1. Calculator 10. Belt Pull

Works out the pull to draw a belt onto or off a conveyor with a winch, or with the conveyor drive when the old belt pulls the new one, section by section along the route and with intermediate pullers where the pull is staged. It gives the peak running and breakaway pull, the winch or drive needs, the least back tension, the let-off brake torque and the clamp loads.
