4. Straight-Warp and Aramid Belting

Straight-warp belts carry load on uncrimped warp yarns in a single integrally woven carcass, which reduces constructional stretch and yarn-on-yarn abrasion and improves rip propagation resistance and impact fatigue life. Aramid belts are manufactured by the same process.

5. Solid Woven PVC Belting

Solid woven belts have an integrally woven carcass saturated with PVC and fused by heat. The interlocked weave has no plies to separate, and the construction is widely used for FRAS-certified underground coal belting.

Table 6: Solid Woven PVC Belt Manufacture
Stage Process Critical Controls Defect if Uncontrolled
WeavingA three-dimensional loom weaves polyester or polyamide warp yarns, polyamide weft yarns and binder yarns into a single interlocked carcass. Carcass thickness is typically 5 to 15 mm [0.2 to 0.6 in] for 400 to 1,800 kN/m [2,300 to 10,300 PIW]. Cotton binder yarns absorb plastisol and improve PVC bonding and fastener retention. All-synthetic binders reduce moisture absorption and resist biological degradation.Weave density and binder interlockLow fastener retention. Weave distortion.
ImpregnationThe carcass is impregnated with PVC plastisol by vacuum impregnation after weaving, or by wet weaving, in which the warp yarns are impregnated during or before weaving. Full core saturation of the dense carcass is the primary process difficulty.Core saturationDry core. Internal moisture wicking.

PVC degradation at excess temperature. High belt elongation.
CoversPVC covers are coated onto the impregnated carcass, gelled and pressed. PVC and nitrile rubber blend (PVG) covers are used where higher drive friction and abrasion resistance are required. Nitrile is compatible with PVC and fuses to the impregnated carcass in a press.Cover-to-carcass adhesionCover delamination

FRAS certification applies to the complete belt construction. A change of binder yarn, impregnation compound or cover compound requires recertification. For underground belts, request sectioned samples demonstrating full core impregnation.

9. Factory Acceptance Testing

Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) verifies finished belt integrity against the purchase specification. Manufacturing defects, including uneven cord tension, entrapped air and incorrect cure, cannot be rectified in situ and shorten belt life.

State the required tests, sampling frequency and acceptance criteria in the purchase specification. Destructive tests are performed on samples cut from the belt before shipment.
