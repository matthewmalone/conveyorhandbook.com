1. Calculator 9. Belt Rating Converter

Converts a belt rating between breaking strength and working tension in kN/m, N/mm, PIW and kgf/cm, and names the equivalent fabric, solid woven and steel cord classes. It gives the allowable working and peak tension for the splice and duty, or the class a belt tension needs, with the minimum pulley diameters, troughing limit, belt mass, top cover for the duty and cover grade equivalents.
